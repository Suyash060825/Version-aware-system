Veritas: Version-Aware Enterprise Policy Intelligence
via Incremental Knowledge Compilation and
Adaptive Multi-Tier Retrieval
Suyash Pradhan

Abstract—Enterprise policy repositories undergo continuous
revision while historical and current provisions must coexist in
queryable form. Conventional RAG architectures rank semantic
similarity without temporal validity or authorization scope,
introducing four operational risks: temporal version inversion, fullcorpus re-indexing overhead, generative latency on deterministic
lookups, and scope leakage. Veritas addresses all four through
a composite eligibility predicate E(c, u, tq ) = V (c, tq ) · A(c, u)
enforced before retrieval, a seven-stage SHA-256 incremental
compiler, an adaptive four-tier query router, and an evidence
verification and governance layer. On a 21-policy, 128-chunk,
373-QA enterprise corpus with a 301-query benchmark, Veritas
achieves 100.0% authorization-decision correctness across all
queries (versus 46.84% for an unrestricted baseline), and
answered-query version-selection accuracy of 86.30% (local) and
89.53% (cloud). The lower complete-version accuracy (33.33%)
reflects correct safe abstentions on unauthorized or low-confidence
queries. The incremental compiler achieves 722.50× speedup
over full rebuild (3.57 ms vs. 2,579.31 ms, hash no-op, O(|∆| · d)
complexity). Citation precision reaches 0.7973. Isotonic calibration
reduces ECE from 0.5840 to 0.000 on the calibration split (Brier
score: 0.5855 to 0.1839). Cache isolation over 30 queries reports
0.00% stale, wrong-version, and cross-scope responses. Adversarial
refusal accuracy reaches 88.89% (local) and 100.0% (cloud).
Index Terms—Retrieval-augmented generation, temporal information retrieval, version-aware retrieval, incremental indexing,
enterprise knowledge management, adaptive query routing,
confidence calibration, natural language inference.

A. Hardware, Software, and Corpus
Experiments used an isolated Linux host (kernel 7.1.8100.fc43.x86 64, x86 64) with 12 logical CPU threads (6
physical cores), 31.06 GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX
3050 Laptop GPU with 3.68 GB VRAM. The software stack
comprised Python 3.14.6, PyTorch 2.13.0, ChromaDB 1.5.9,
FAISS 1.15.0, FastEmbed 0.8.0, FlashRank 0.2.10, scikitlearn 1.9.0, and PostgreSQL 16 with Redis 7.
The constructed enterprise-style corpus contains 21 policies
across 8 functional departments, 23 policy versions, 128
authoritative structural chunks, 33 relational facts, and 373
canonical question-answer pairs. Ten simulated enterprise
users provide distinct permission scopes and confidentiality
levels. The held-out benchmark contains 301 queries across
nine operational categories (Table II). Production application
logic was frozen at commit 61d64b71.
B. Metrics and Evaluation Protocol
Version-selection accuracy is measured as the fraction of
queries for which the predicted policy version matches the
gold reference, reported over all queries (Overall) and over the
answered subset (excluding abstentions). Generation quality
uses exact-match percentage, token F1, and citation precision,
recall, and F1. Route latency is measured at P50, P95, and
P99. Calibration uses Brier score and Expected Calibration
Error (ECE) with post-hoc isotonic regression. Cache isolation

is evaluated over a 30-query non-repeating sequence. NLI
verification is reported as a confusion-matrix pilot (n=14 pairs).
Stratified ablation operates on a 30-query mixed operational
subset.

VII. D ISCUSSION

F. Stratified Ablation
Table VIII shows the 30-query mixed operational subset
ablation. The principal finding is that removal of the knowledge
compiler (A1) forces all 30 queries to autoregressive generation,
increasing LLM calls from 0 to 30 while reducing Answer
F1 from 41.28% to 28.51% and Citation F1 from 30.62%
to 10.0%. All other removals leave Answer F1 and Citation
F1 unchanged because neighboring routes provide coverage;
latency changes reflect route redistribution rather than quality
improvement. Lower latency in some ablated configurations
does not represent a system improvement because route
composition changed. Ablations A2–A7 demonstrate the value
of the tiered architecture: removing any individual path does not
degrade output quality because the router redirects to the next
capable tier, but does increase median latency (A2: 107.07 ms,
A3: 111.1 ms, A5: 112.59 ms) as traffic falls through to slower
paths. The compiler (A1) is the only non-redundant component:
it has no fallback tier and its removal forces all queries to
autoregressive generation.

Authorization and calibration as enterprise invariants.
The Composite Eligibility Predicate E(c, u, tq ) = V · A is
enforced at every tier via the immutable QueryScope dataclass; no downstream component can bypass it. In compliance
settings, false abstentions (refusals on answerable queries) are
preferable to false assertions (citing superseded clauses). The
isotonic confidence gate formalizes this asymmetric cost by
shrinking confidence toward the calibration prior. Runtime ECE
of 0.2954 versus calibration-split ECE of 0.000 indicates a
distribution gap requiring continued calibration coverage.
VIII. L IMITATIONS AND T HREATS TO VALIDITY
1) Corpus scale. The 21-policy, 128-chunk corpus is constructed; generalization to larger, heterogeneous corpora
remains undemonstrated.

2) Benchmark category skew. 89.37% of queries target
deterministic paths, reducing direct evidence for openended enterprise dialogue.
3) Citation granularity. Citation recall gap (0.2487) reflects section-level chunks versus sentence-level expected
annotations.
4) Incremental delta ratio. Evaluated update is a hash noop; speedups at 10%–50% delta ratios with re-embedding
are unmeasured.
5) NLI pilot scale. Validation set (n = 14) is pilot-scale;
n ≥ 50 is recommended for statistical confidence.
6) Model quantization. Exact-match (27.91%) and token F1
(0.3516) are bounded by 4-bit quantization of qwen3:4b.
7) Hardware dependence. Latency measurements are
hardware-specific (12 vCPU / RTX 3050 Laptop).
8) Tier-3 coverage. Version-comparison handled ≤ 1
benchmark query; dedicated evaluation is deferred to
future work.
9) Cache throughput. Non-repeating test sequence yielded
0% hits; steady-state throughput remains unestablished.
10) Calibration gap. Calibration-split ECE (0.000) versus
runtime ECE (0.2954) indicates a distribution gap.
11) Adversarial testing. Adversarial category contains 5
queries; broader injection and jailbreak testing is unevaluated.
IX. C ONCLUSION
Veritas integrates temporal validity modeling, role-based
authorization,
incremental SHA-256 knowledge compilation, adaptive
four-tier retrieval, DeBERTa-v3 evidence verification, and
governance-oriented auditability into a single enterprise policy
intelligence architecture. On a 21-policy, 128-chunk, 373canonical-QA corpus, the system achieves answered-query
version-selection accuracy of 86.30% (local) and 89.53%
(cloud) over a 301-query benchmark. The incremental compiler
achieves 722.50× speedup over full rebuild in the hash noop scenario, with O(|∆| · d) complexity for the general case.
Deterministic routes (Tier-0 and Tier-1) serve 54.15% of queries
at a P50 below 24 ms, driving the composite system median
to 29.65 ms. Isotonic calibration reduces ECE by 100% on
the calibration split. Cache isolation testing records zero stale,
wrong-version, or cross-scope responses. Future work targets
larger multi-domain corpora, a purpose-built version-diff query
set, incremental compilation evaluation at non-trivial delta
ratios, expanded NLI validation (n ≥ 50), and productionscale generative evaluation under full-precision models.
