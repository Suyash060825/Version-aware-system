Policy Knowledge Graph

A map of how your company's policies actually came to be — built directly from real records, not guesses. It connects Departments → Policies → Versions → Approvals, and Meetings → Decisions → Policies, so you can trace any policy back to the meeting and decision that produced it, and forward to who signed off on it.

▶ How do I read this map? (start here)
Think of this as a family tree for a policy. Somebody had a meeting (purple) → in that meeting, a decision was made (red) → that decision produced a policy (blue) → the policy belongs to a department (green) → over time the policy gets edited, creating new versions (grey) → and each version needed someone to approve it (yellow) before it became official.

Every box and dot below is a real record from the database — nothing here is invented by AI. Click any shape to read a plain-English explanation of what it is, on the right. Drag to move things around, scroll to zoom, and use the search box to jump straight to a policy by name.
Meeting
A real meeting where a policy topic was discussed. This is where the trail begins.
Decision
The specific choice made in that meeting that led to a policy being created or changed.
Policy
The official rule employees have to follow. Shown as a box so it stands out.
Department
The team responsible for a policy, such as HR or IT.
Version
A specific edition of a policy. New versions appear whenever a policy is edited.
Approval
A sign-off a version needed, and who gave it, before it became official.
